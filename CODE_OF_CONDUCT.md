# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ShipProof welcomes respectful, specific, and evidence-led participation. Constructive disagreement about severity, confidence, architecture, research methods, and project direction is part of healthy technical review.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- giving actionable feedback and acknowledging uncertainty;
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and communication styles;
- protecting credentials, personal data, private source code, and embargoed vulnerability details;
- correcting mistakes without retaliation or humiliation.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, impersonation, and publication of another person's secrets or private material without authorization.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, reviews, release discussions, project-managed community spaces, and private project communications. It also applies when a person represents ShipProof in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Do not include live credentials, unnecessary personal data, or public vulnerability details. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, issue a private warning, limit participation, or remove access when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, document the relevant behavior, and avoid conflicts of interest. Good-faith security reports, accessibility feedback, and technical disagreement are not conduct violations.
